# Design analysis and adaptation

## Reference characteristics observed

The reference site uses a cinematic production-interface language: a loading intro, timecode/recording labels, enormous condensed type, mostly monochrome presentation, precise microcopy, section numbering, moving marquees and restrained animation. Its content structure moves from a dramatic identity statement into studio information, people, selected projects, services and a strong final contact statement.

## What this build borrows at the pattern level

- Film-production interface cues such as timecode and recording status
- Large editorial typography paired with tiny technical labels
- Numbered content chapters
- A dark, premium, cinematic atmosphere
- Slow reveal motion, marquees and hover interactions
- A portfolio-first narrative with a large closing CTA

## What is intentionally original

- New brand: FRAME//SHIFT
- New copy, taglines, project names and service descriptions
- Different section order: thesis → work → capabilities → method → contact
- Generative signal-network hero rather than a recreated reference hero
- Outline/solid split headline composition
- CSS-built fictional campaign art instead of copied videos or images
- Light editorial process section to create contrast and pacing
- Different color system with ice-cyan, acid and warm signal accents
- Different card proportions, interaction model and responsive behavior

## Design system

- Background: near-black `#0b0d0d`
- Primary text: warm off-white `#efeee8`
- Main accent: ice cyan `#caf6ff`
- Supporting status colors: acid green and signal orange
- Typography: system sans for speed and portability, Georgia italic for cinematic contrast
- Motion: long ease-out reveals, low-amplitude parallax, continuous timecode and ambient canvas movement
