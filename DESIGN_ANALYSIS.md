# CK Filmz Phase 1 Adaptation Notes

The original FRAME//SHIFT concept has been adapted into a real portfolio-first website for CK Filmz.

## Preserved visual language
- Dark cinematic interface
- Timecode and production-status motifs
- Oversized editorial typography
- Ice-cyan accent colour
- Motion-led loading sequence
- Scroll reveals, marquee, custom cursor and generative canvas

## Phase 1 business goal
The site is now built to support portfolio credibility and project inquiries, not e-commerce. It positions CK Filmz for UAE and international clients by leading with high-end visual work, AI content creation, film production and lead-generation marketing capability.

## Future-ready structure
- Portfolio and service inquiry now
- Legal/license identity can be added in footer and legal pages
- Domain can move to `chetankapadnis.com`
- Products or packaged services can later become a Shop page or separate subdomain
